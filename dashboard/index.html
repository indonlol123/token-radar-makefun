How a row is priced

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity

The category multiple is the median mcap/TVL of the live-token protocols in that category (at least 3 priced samples; a $0 market cap carries no price signal and is left out). A thinner category falls back to the global median over every priced live token. Multiples are clamped to 0.05×–1.00×. Growth is the 7-day TVL change clamped to −60%..+120% at half weight. Maturity runs 0.60 for a listing of yesterday to 1.00 after three years with no token. Nothing here is keyed to a protocol's name.
